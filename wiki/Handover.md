## Scope of these pages

These pages cover the **technical** handover only: systems, access, knowledge and documentation.

Commercial, contractual and account-structure matters are deliberately not recorded in this wiki.
This wiki is a technical record and its readership will grow over time; anything sensitive to the
commercial arrangement belongs elsewhere and stays with the people responsible for it.

## The principle

> No AFMA system may depend on knowledge held by one person.

Every item on the checklist exists to move one piece of knowledge out of someone's head and into a
place the team can reach. A task is not done when someone has explained something. It is done when it
is written here and a second person has followed the written version successfully.
